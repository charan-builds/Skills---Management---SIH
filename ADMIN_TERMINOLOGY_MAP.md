# Admin Terminology Standardization Map

This document establishes the official terminology mappings for the Admin / Government Panel of the Skilling Impact Intelligence Platform, enforcing clear, accessible, and decision-oriented language across all screens, navigation items, and reports.

---

## 1. Master Terminology Mapping Table

| Internal / Technical Term | Approved User-Facing Label | Analytical Meaning & Intent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `Outcome Overview` | **Programme Outcomes** | Macro evaluation of skilling outcomes including completion, placement, self-employment, and overall absorption into the formal economy. |
| `Employment Outcome Tracking` | **Employment Over Time** | Longitudinal retention curves tracking candidates across Day 1, 3-Month, 6-Month, and 12-Month checkpoints against state benchmarks. |
| `Wage Progression` | **Income Growth** | Longitudinal tracking of monthly earnings expansion from initial corporate placement through merit performance increments over 12 months. |
| `Skill Gap Analysis` | **Skills Missing in Jobs** | Identification of specific technical competencies where employer demand exceeds observed candidate proficiency. |
| `Demand vs Supply` | **Industry Demand vs Training** | Direct comparison between corporate job opening requirements and qualified vocational graduates trained in the state. |
| `Curriculum-Skill Mapping` | **Skills Taught vs Skills Needed** | Audit comparing institutional syllabus modules and target benchmark proficiencies against observed workplace evaluation scores. |
| `Training Relevance Analysis` | **Training Relevance** | Evaluation matrix scoring how closely daily job responsibilities match the vocational curriculum taught to candidates. |
| `Non-Placement Analysis` | **Why People Don't Get Jobs** | Diagnostic root-cause investigation identifying why certified vocational graduates experience placement barriers. |
| `Attrition Analysis` | **Why People Leave Jobs** | Diagnostic investigation of post-placement departures, distinguishing between lateral career growth and premature resignation. |
| `Provider Accountability` | **Training Provider Performance** | Comparative accountability ranking of accredited vocational partners across completion rates, pass rates, placement, and 6M retention. |
| `District Analytics` | **District Performance** | Spatial evaluation of skilling absorption, local employment rates, and regional competency deficits across administrative districts. |
| `Cohort Analysis` | **Cohort Comparison** | Comparative analysis across consecutive training quarters and years to identify improving or declining quality trajectories. |
| `Programme Evaluation` | **Programme Performance** | Deep evaluation of specific skilling courses to identify lagging outcomes, skill deficits, and curriculum intervention points. |
| `Impact Intelligence` | **Key Findings & Insights** | Algorithmic policy findings highlighting critical placement deficits, retention bottlenecks, and recommended state interventions. |
| `Employer Verification Oversight` | **Employer Verification** | Automated HRIS telemetry and manual review queues confirming candidate employment status, joining dates, and wages. |
| `Verification Exceptions` | **Verification Issues** | Detection of corporate employment conflicts, salary mismatches, or disputed resignation records requiring review. |

## 3. Phrasing Principles for Analytical Explanations
To prevent unsupported causal claims while maintaining analytical rigor, all automated explanations adhere to these guidelines:

- **Use Grounded Phrasing**:
  - *"Associated factors include..."*
  - *"Observed pattern demonstrates..."*
  - *"Potential contributing factors identified across employer reviews..."*
- **Avoid Absolute Causal Claims**:
  - ❌ *"Programme X caused candidates to fail interviews."*
  - ✅ *"Lower interview clearance in Programme X is strongly associated with curriculum deficits in hands-on cloud lab simulators."*
