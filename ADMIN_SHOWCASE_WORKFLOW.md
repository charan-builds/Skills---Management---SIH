# Admin Showcase Workflow & Live Demo Guide

This document defines the exact **18-Step Live Presentation Workflow** demonstrating how the Admin Panel operates as an integrated **Outcome Intelligence and Evaluation System**—from macro population statistics down to granular individual candidate journeys and back.

---

## 18-Step Demonstration Script

### STEP 1: Macro Population Surveillance
1. Navigate to `/admin`.
2. Observe the state overview:
   - **800 Total Trainees** across 5 programmes, 5 providers, and 6 districts.
   - Comprehensive headline KPIs: Certified Pass, Formal Placed, Self-Employed, Apprentices, Employment Rate, 6M Retention Rate, Average Monthly Wage, Follow-up Completion.
3. Highlight that every figure is derived deterministically from the underlying relational records.

---

### STEP 2: Scoping via Global Filters
1. Open the global top filter bar.
2. Select **Programme: Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps**.
3. Point out that the scope count immediately transitions to the exact subset of candidates (~172 trainees).
4. All executive KPI cards, funnel metrics, wage numbers, and charts instantly re-aggregate.

---

### STEP 3: Programme Performance Evaluation
1. Click **Programme Performance** in the sidebar (`/admin/programmes`).
2. Select **Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps** to inspect its comprehensive evaluation.
3. Observe:
   - Funnel conversion stages (Enrolled $\rightarrow$ Certified $\rightarrow$ Placed)
   - Wage growth curve across checkpoints
   - Top skill gaps, non-placement factors, and attrition drivers.

---

### STEP 4: Inspecting "Why People Don't Get Jobs"
1. Click **Programme Outcomes** (`/admin/outcomes`).
2. Scroll to the section **Why People Don't Get Jobs**.
3. Point out the interactive Donut chart displaying the distribution of non-placement barriers:
   - *Skills*, *Jobs in District*, *Location*, *Salary*, *Experience*, *Education*, *Other*.

---

### STEP 5: Root-Cause Drilldown — Location Relocation Constraints
1. In the Donut chart or category pills, click **Location / Relocation Constraints**.
2. Observe the instant expansion of the **Diagnostic Detail Panel**:
   - Total affected candidates and percentage of unplaced group.
   - Geographic distribution showing concentration in Tier-2 districts.
   - Evidence-derived analytical synthesis:
     > *"Relocation constraints are observed disproportionately in Tier-2 districts within Cloud Infrastructure. 68% of candidates in this group report lack of regional hostel subsidies and entry-level commute support."*
   - Sample candidate dossiers with candidate IDs and reported barrier statements.

---

### STEP 6: Inspecting "Why People Leave Jobs"
1. Scroll down to **Why People Leave Jobs**.
2. Point out the Donut chart displaying reported departure drivers:
   - *Low Initial Compensation*, *Better Opportunity*, *Skill Mismatch*, *Working Conditions*, *Relocation*, *Contract End*, *Personal*.

---

### STEP 7: Pattern Analysis — Salary-Related Exits
1. Click the **Salary (Low Initial Compensation)** segment or pill.
2. Observe the **Pattern Analysis Panel**:
   - Exit salary vs. platform average wage (identifying an 18% compensation gap).
   - Concentration by programme, district, and training provider.
   - Telemetry finding:
     > *"Salary-related exits are concentrated in Cloud Infrastructure and Pune. Average exit salary of ₹24,200 is 18% lower than retained peers in the same domain."*

---

### STEP 8: Identifying Skills Missing in Jobs
1. Click **Skills Missing in Jobs** in the sidebar (`/admin/skill-gaps`).
2. Identify the top competency deficit:
   - **Kubernetes & Containerization** (affecting 90+ trainees and reported by major employers).

---

### STEP 9: Auditing Skills Taught vs Skills Needed
1. Scroll to **Skills Taught vs Skills Needed** (Curriculum Audit).
2. Examine module `MOD-103 Docker & Kubernetes`:
   - Target proficiency benchmark: **85%**.
   - Observed workplace score: **68%** (-17% deficit status).
3. Demonstrate that the platform correctly flags this as a critical syllabus deficiency.

---

### STEP 10: Industry Demand vs Training
1. Scroll to the **Industry Demand vs Training** matrix.
2. Observe that employer demand for Containerization stands at **280 openings**, while vocational training supply is only **190 candidates** (net state deficit of 90 engineers).

---

### STEP 11: Training Provider Performance
1. Click **Training Provider Performance** (`/admin/providers`).
2. Compare provider metrics:
   - Note high-performing partners (e.g. *TATA STRIVE* with 86% placement and 83% retention) against lower-scoring centers.
   - Point out wage growth metrics computed from employer verification feeds.

---

### STEP 12: District Performance
1. Click **District Performance** (`/admin/districts`).
2. Compare metropolitan clusters (*Mumbai*, *Pune*) against Tier-2 districts (*Nagpur*, *Nashik*, *Guntur*).
3. Highlight regional skill gaps and absorption differences.

---

### STEP 13: Cohort Comparison
1. Click **Cohort Comparison** (`/admin/cohorts`).
2. Review the four quarterly cohorts (*2023-Q2* through *2024-Q1*).
3. Demonstrate how completion rates, pass rates, and 6-month retention have evolved longitudinally.

---

### STEP 14: Population Exploration in Trainee Directory
1. Click **Trainees** in the sidebar (`/admin/trainees`).
2. In the search box, search for a candidate (e.g., `"Aarav"` or `"TR-0001"` or by employer `"Tata Consultancy Services"`).
3. Filter by **Current Outcome: Employed** and **Retention: Retained**.
4. Observe the clean, privacy-safe table displaying masked email/phone and verified wages.

---

### STEP 15: Individual Longitudinal Profile Deep-Dive
1. Click **View Profile** on candidate `TR-0001`.
2. Inspect the complete individual intelligence view:
   - **Outcome Summary Matrix**: Training completed, certified, employed, 6M retained, wage progression ₹28,000 $\rightarrow$ ₹33,040 (+18%).
   - **Longitudinal Journey Timeline**: Click on milestone nodes (e.g., *Job Joined*, *3-Month Check-in*, *Wage Increment*) to view verified event telemetry.
   - **Last 6 Months Activity**: Chronological feed of recent verification, wage updates, and employer evaluations.
   - **Multi-Job Employment History**: Career progression showing initial placement and lateral transitions.
   - **Income / Wage Progression**: Interactive line chart graphing salary growth.
   - **Follow-up & Retention Checks**: Verified 3M/6M/12M evaluations.
   - **Skills & Competency Matrix**: Taught skills vs used skills vs missing competencies.

---

### STEP 16: Connecting Individual Data to Aggregate Analytics
1. Show how `TR-0001`'s specific records directly roll up into:
   - The provider's placement percentage.
   - The programme's wage growth rate.
   - The district's retention benchmark.
2. Emphasize that clicking any aggregate category traces directly back to candidate dossiers.

---

### STEP 17: Reviewing Key Findings & Insights
1. Click **Key Findings & Insights** (`/admin/interventions`).
2. Review structured insight cards:
   - *Technical Interview Deficit in Cloud Infrastructure*
   - *Early Month-4 Attrition in EV Automotive Assembly*
   - *Critical Shortage of Clinical ICU & Triage Staff*
   - *Tier-2 Location Mismatch in Guntur & Nagpur*
   - *Above-Average Wage Growth in Full Stack Engineering*
3. Click **View Traceable Evidence** on any card to open the **Evidence Dossier** detailing reported gap counts, employer quotes, and supply/demand gaps.
4. Click **Adopt Action** to demonstrate interactive state mutation.

---

### STEP 18: Generating the Audit Report
1. Click **Reports** (`/admin/reports`).
2. Click **Generate Formal Audit Report**.
3. Verify that the generated executive report and downloadable payload reflect the exact filtered scope.
